About DAV Institution

Nurturing Leaders with Character & Competence

DAV Public School stands as a beacon of progressive education, combining rigorous academic curriculum with moral integrity and spiritual grounding.

Inspired by the ideals of Swami Dayanand Saraswati, our institution fosters an environment where curiosity is encouraged, critical thinking is sharpened, and compassionate citizenship is cultivated. We prepare our students not just for examinations, but for life's grand challenges.
