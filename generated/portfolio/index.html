Hello! I'm Alex Rivera, a Senior Full Stack Engineer based in San Francisco with over 8 years of professional experience building web applications, scalable cloud microservices, and interactive developer tools.

My journey began when I built my first custom web crawler in high school. Since then, I've led development teams at fast-growing fintech startups, consulted for Enterprise Fortune 500 clients, and contributed heavily to open-source developer ecosystems.

I specialize in modern JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystems (React, Next.js, Node.js), combined with robust backend services in Python and PostgreSQL. I thrive at the intersection of slick UI design and bulletproof software engineering.
